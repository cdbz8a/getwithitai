    Eight categories

    80 and up is strong. 60 to 79 is usable. 40 to 59 is thin. Under 40 needs a role and a format. Same prompt always gets the same number.

    “You are…” names a voice. A real job title is enough.

    Specificity · 15

    Numbers, names, audiences. Skip words like “nice,” “stuff,” “engaging.”

    Structure · 15

    Headings, bullets, tags, or line breaks so the task is scannable.

    Output format · 10

    Table, bullets, email, or a word cap. One shape.

    Limits · 10

    Do not / avoid / never / limit. Leave prices and names blank unless you typed them.
