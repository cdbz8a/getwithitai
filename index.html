Who it's for

Anyone whose day fills up with email and paperwork.

Office and admin staff

If you spend your day on email and minutes, AI can hand you a first draft of each one in seconds. You fix it up and send it, and you're usually done well before you would have been.

Managers and leadership

Briefing notes, funding proposals, reports to council or the board, and the hard letter you keep putting off. You still make every decision. AI takes the first pass at the writing so you start out editing.

Small business and side work

Quotes, follow-ups and social posts usually get done late at night or on a Sunday. With AI they take a few minutes between jobs.

Anyone who feels behind

You keep hearing about AI and nobody has ever sat down and shown you how to use it. That's what this course does, one step at a time, in plain language.

Questions

Questions people ask.

I'm not a computer person. Can I do this?

You'll be fine. If you can send a text message, you can do this. The whole course is in plain English, and you practice on your own emails and paperwork from the first hour.

Which AI app do I need?

The free version of ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini or Copilot is plenty to start with. They all run in a web browser or a phone app. Module 4 helps you pick one, and the free lessons link to all of them.

How do I pay?

You register with the form on this site. We email you back from getwithitai@protonmail.com with the dates and payment details. If some other address or link ever asks you to pay, email us first and check.

What about private and work information?

There's a free lesson and a full module on this. Names, addresses, member and account numbers, and anything confidential stay out of public AI tools. You'll learn the placeholder method, where you swap private details for stand-ins, get your draft, and then put the real details back in on your own computer. It takes about thirty seconds.

Will this actually stick?

That's what the 18-day challenge is for. After the course you practice one skill a day on your own work, and your instructor checks in along the way. By day 18 it's part of how you work, and you've built a prompt library you'll keep using.

Can you train our whole office?

Yes. Private sessions run for up to 15 people and use examples from your own workplace. Email getwithitai@protonmail.com with your headcount and what your team does.
