Questions

Questions people ask.

I already use ChatGPT every day. Will this be too basic?

Probably not. Most daily users have never been shown how to structure a brief, use roles and examples, build templates, manage a long conversation's context or set up a checking routine. That's where consistent results come from, and it's most of the day. If you want to check the level first, the free 28 minutes is a good sample.

I'm new to this. Will I keep up?

Yes. Module 1 assumes you've never used an AI app, everything is in plain English, and you practice on your own tasks from the first hour. If you can send a text message, you can do this.

Is it just ChatGPT?

No. We cover ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, Copilot and Perplexity, what each one is best at, and when a paid plan is worth it. The free versions are enough for the whole course.

What about private and confidential information?

There's a full module on it. You'll learn what never goes into a public AI tool, the placeholder method for working safely with sensitive material, and how to work within your organization's AI policy.

Will this actually stick?

That's what the 18-day challenge is for. You practice one skill a day on your own tasks, your instructor checks in, and by day 18 you've got a routine and a prompt library built from your own work.
