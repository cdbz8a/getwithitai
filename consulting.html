A typical engagement

Three weeks, in plain terms

Week 1. Listen

Which three jobs eat the week. Which tools are already allowed. Where private data sits. We write it down and send it back so you can correct us.

Week 2. Build

A short workflow per role: the repeating task, the prompt, the human check. One written process if knowledge is walking out with people.

Week 3. Train

People use their real work. Then a written pack they can reopen on Monday.

Timelines stretch for bigger groups. We say so in the quote.

Who this is for

  • Shops and offices of roughly 3 to 15 people who share the same customer work.
  • Managers who want a short set of rules instead of “just try ChatGPT.”
  • Community and First Nations groups who need careful handling of knowledge. See Knowledge Keeper.
Do you install software?

We work with the tools you already pay for (Microsoft 365, Google Workspace, a chat assistant your IT will allow). We are trainers and process people.

Will you train on our private files?

Work that goes into a public model is redacted first: Role A, Client B, ranges instead of exact invoices. If the work cannot be redacted, it stays off public tools.
