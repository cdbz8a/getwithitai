Libraries · 18 prompts

Short copy prompts

Signs, captions, subjects. Ban-list on every card.

1. 30-word caption

You are a local writer. 30-word Instagram caption for [OFFER]. Name [PLACE]. No hashtag dump. Avoid delve, landscape, leverage.

2. Subject line

Email subject, 6 words max, about [OFFER], no ALL CAPS, no fake urgency.

3. Window sign

8 words max, one line, [OFFER], no comma if it still reads.

4. SMS

Under 160 characters. [WHO] is [WHEN] out. Need [ACCESS]. Sound like a person.

5. Sold-out

20 words. We sold out of [ITEM]. Next tray [TIME]. No apology novel.

6. Hours change

40 words. New hours [HOURS] starting [DATE]. One reason. No corporate we-are-excited.

7. Hiring

60 words. Hiring [ROLE] in [TOWN]. One duty, one requirement, how to apply. No culture-fit fog.

8. Event

40 words. [EVENT] at [PLACE], [DAY], [TIME]. What to bring. No firework emojis.

9. Price without fake discounts

Name the price [PRICE] in one sentence. What it includes. No crossed-out fake MSRP.

10. Thank-you

40 words. Thanks for [THING]. Next step [STEP]. No 'don't hesitate.'

11. Rain delay

Two sentences. Outdoor job delayed. Next window [DAY] if dry. No blame.

12. Ban-list only pass

Delete: delve, landscape, leverage, tapestry, robust, seamless, unlock, elevate, game-changer. Keep meaning. [PASTE]

13. Voicemail script

20 seconds spoken. [WHO] calling about [JOB]. One callback number. No "touching base."

14. Review reply

Public reply to this review. 50 words. Thank them for the specific thing they named. One next step. No arguing in public. [PASTE REVIEW]

15. Gift-card promo

40 words. Gift cards for [OCCASION]. Where to buy. Expiry if you provided one. Do not invent expiry.

16. After-hours auto-reply

SMS auto-reply. We're closed. Open [HOURS]. For emergencies: [RULE]. One question they can answer so we triage in the morning.

17. Two options, no pressure

80 words. Option A and Option B for [JOB]. What's included in each. Ask which they prefer. No "only 2 spots left."

18. Plain bio

40-word bio for [PERSON], [TRADE] in [TOWN]. Years only if provided. No "passionate about excellence."
