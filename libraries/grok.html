Libraries · 10 prompts

Grok prompts

First two lines are the job. No preamble.

1. Cut the draft

Cut this to 90 words. Keep the offer. Kill delve/landscape/leverage. No new facts. [PASTE DRAFT]

2. Window sign

Write an 8-word window sign for [SHOP]. One line. Readable from the sidewalk. No puns.

3. Blunt recap

6 bullets. Verbs first. No owners you were not given. No pep. [PASTE NOTES]

4. Name strike

Give 10 names for [BUSINESS] in [PLACE]. Then strike 5 that sound generic and say why in 5 words each.

5. JSON caption

JSON only, keys headline, body, cta. headline max 60 chars. body max 180. cta one of Shop, Book, Call. No fence.

6. Ban list rewrite

Rewrite. Avoid: delve, landscape, leverage, tapestry, robust, seamless, unlock, elevate. [PASTE]

7. One question

Ask the reader one useful question at the end. 70 words. About [TOPIC]. No hashtags.

8. Compare without a winner unless asked

Table: Option / Fits if / Breaks if. Three options: [A], [B], [C]. No fake prices.

9. Strip the greeting

Delete the throat-clearing. Start at the job. Keep meaning. [PASTE]

10. Local line

One sentence that names [TOWN] and [HOURS]. No slogan.
