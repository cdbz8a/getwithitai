Libraries · 12 prompts

Still image prompts

Photoreal stills. No on-image type unless you add it yourself.

1. On-white hero

Subject: [PRODUCT], materials honest, no invented logos. Style: photoreal studio. Light: soft even, faint contact shadow. Framing: 50mm, slight three-quarter, product fills 70%. Background: pure white seamless. No on-image text.

2. Window-light kitchen

Subject: [PRODUCT] on a worn oak board. Style: photoreal documentary. Light: camera-left morning window, no fill. Framing: 35mm, table height. Background: kitchen wall, no people, no readable labels.

3. Outdoor dusk

Subject: [PRODUCT] on a cedar railing. Style: photoreal. Light: dusk, warm sky, no flash. Framing: 50mm, eye level. Background: out-of-focus trees. No logos.

4. Macro detail

Subject: grain and edge of [MATERIAL]. Style: photoreal macro. Light: hard raking from camera-left. Framing: 90mm, fill the frame. Background: falloff to dark. No text.

5. Hands off

Subject: [PRODUCT] alone. Style: photoreal. Light: soft. Framing: 50mm. Constraint: no hands, no people, no extra props unless named.

6. Colour lock

Subject: [PRODUCT]. Style: photoreal. Palette only: [COLOUR A] and [COLOUR B]. Light: camera-left window. Framing: 50mm. Background: [COLOUR B] seamless.

7. Overhead flat lay

Subject: [PRODUCT] and two named props. Style: photoreal overhead. Light: even, no harsh corners. Framing: 35mm, directly above. Background: linen. No type.

8. Wet surface

Subject: [PRODUCT] with real water beads. Style: photoreal. Light: soft window. Framing: 50mm. Background: slate. No fake sparkles, no extra bottles.

9. Night practical

Subject: [PRODUCT] beside a lit lamp. Style: photoreal interior night. Light: practical lamp only. Framing: 35mm. Background: dark room, no TV logos.

10. Snow / cold

Subject: [PRODUCT] on packed snow. Style: photoreal. Light: overcast. Framing: 50mm. Background: treeline, no footprints unless asked, no text.

11. Black field

Subject: [PRODUCT], polished metal. Style: photoreal. Light: single softbox camera-left, specular controlled. Framing: 85mm. Background: true black. No reflections of studio gear.

12. Lifestyle, three props max

Subject: [PRODUCT] as hero. Setting: [ROOM]. Surface: [SURFACE]. Light: natural. Props: at most three, named. Framing: 50mm. No people, no readable packaging type.
