Libraries · 10 prompts

Short clip prompts

One move. A few seconds. Start from a still when you can.

1. Slow push

[Slow push-in]: [PRODUCT] on [SURFACE], morning window from camera-left, 5 seconds, no tilt, no people, no on-screen text.

2. Gentle pan

[Slow pan right]: [SCENE], locked exposure, 5 seconds, one practical light, no extra action.

3. Steam only

Start on the still. Steam rises from [OBJECT]. Camera locked. 5 seconds. No other motion.

4. Pour

[Locked off]: liquid pours into [VESSEL] for 4 seconds. Real gravity. No slow-motion unless asked. No faces.

5. Orbit small

[Short orbital, 20 degrees]: [PRODUCT] on black, 6 seconds, studio, no logo inventions.

6. Rack nothing

Do not rack focus. Locked still of [SCENE], 5 seconds, ambient only.

7. Three-shot list

1) Wide locked 4s [PLACE]. 2) Insert 3s [HAND-TOOL]. if no hands, skip. 3) Slow push 5s on [PRODUCT]. No titles.

8. Rain window

[Locked]: rain on glass, interior warm, [PRODUCT] on sill, 6 seconds, no music direction, no text.

9. Flag in wind

[Locked]: fabric moves in light wind, 5 seconds, daylight, no extra objects entering frame.

10. End hold

Push in 4 seconds then hold 2 seconds on [PRODUCT]. No fade, no type.
